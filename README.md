## 📖 Synopsis

Scarlett entered prison accused of poisoning her stepsister. There, she gave birth—and was told her baby had died. With nothing left to lose, she joined a military program few inmates survived. Four years after her conviction, she returns as Supreme Commander of the Obsidian Unit. 

Her family sees only the disgraced woman they once cast aside. Scarlett lets them. Keeping her rank concealed, she steps back into the lives of those who framed her, carrying the grief they dismissed and the authority they never imagined. They expect a former prisoner to know her place. They have no idea how much power she now commands.
